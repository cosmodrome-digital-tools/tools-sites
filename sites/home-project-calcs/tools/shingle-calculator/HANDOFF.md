# Handoff: shingle-calculator

## What was built
- Tool slug: shingle-calculator
- Tool name (meta.json "title"): Shingle Calculator (benefit: "Bundles, Starter, Ridge Cap, and Nails"; `seoTitle`: "Shingle Calculator - Bundles and Squares" (set on intake), so the full `<title>` is "Shingle Calculator - Bundles and Squares | Home & Yard Calcs", 59 characters)
- What it calculates, in one sentence: From a roof footprint and pitch (x/12 or a direct multiplier) plus eave, rake, and ridge/hip lengths, it gives roof area, squares, field bundles before and after waste, starter, ridge cap, underlayment rolls, and field nails, plus a shopping list grouped into field / accessories / fasteners.
- Layout / chart style / accent chosen: `stepper` layout with 5 steps (Roof footprint, Roof pitch, Roof edges, Shingles and waste, Advanced: accessory coverage) and a `bar` chart (bundles by type: no waste, with waste, starter, ridge cap). This layout/chart pair wasn't used by the 10 earlier tools. Accent is warm asphalt charcoal `#4a3f36` with an amber "granule" highlight `#b45309`, on a warm off-white `#f8f5f0` with a faint staggered shingle-course pattern. Step badges are notched tabs, the radius is 6px, and the display font is Franklin Gothic/Arial Narrow/system. I nudged the suggested `#44403c` slightly warmer: next to slate `#334155` the two have the same lightness, so the difference has to come from hue (brown vs blue-gray) plus the amber stripe. New `assets/hero.svg` (decorative shingled roof, about 1.3 KB, `heroAlt: ""`).
- Category: Roofing
- Unique addition: the grouped shopping list (rendered under the results by `ui.js` from the pure `shoppingList()` in `logic.js`), the product selector that loads starter, ridge cap, and underlayment coverage from cited GAF and Owens Corning data sheets, and an "Accessory coverage by product line" comparison table in page.md.

### Layout quirks (for intake)
- `ui.js` does UI wiring only, like concrete, paver, and roof pitch: it hides whichever of `pitch` / `multiplier` isn't selected, copies the `PRODUCTS` preset from `logic.js` into the three coverage fields when the product changes, switches the selector to "Custom" when someone types a coverage value, and injects two elements after `.calc-results__list`: `.shingle-list` (the shopping list) and `.shingle-note` (the low-slope note, empty unless the pitch is 2/12 to under 4/12).
- The frame has no collapsible "Advanced" accordion, so the advanced inputs are their own last step, labeled "Advanced: accessory coverage" (the same pattern rebar uses).
- `logic.js` validates only the pitch field that's in use, so a junk value in the hidden field never blocks results.
- Zip path: `shingle-calculator.zip` at the repo root, made with `Compress-Archive -Path sites/home-project-calcs/tools/shingle-calculator -DestinationPath shingle-calculator.zip -Force`. It contains the folder `shingle-calculator/` at the top level, **not** the full `sites/home-project-calcs/tools/` path that docs section 10's `zip -r` produces. Unzip it into `sites/home-project-calcs/tools/`.

## Decisions made (choice, source)
Updated on Web Dev intake (2026-10-07). Items marked *(intake)* were changed or added then.
- Choice: Roof area = footprint (with overhangs) × pitch multiplier, M = √(1 + (x/12)²). This works for gable and hip roofs with one pitch throughout. | Source: geometry (Pythagorean slope factor), tested; same method as [Extreme How-To](https://extremehowto.com/calculating-roofing-shingles/) and IKO's [Blueprint for Roofing](https://www.iko.com/na/blog/blueprint-for-roofing/) (attic floor area plus overhangs).
- Choice: The footprint is measured including overhangs (stated in the help text). | Source: shingles cover the eave and rake overhangs; [IKO Blueprint for Roofing](https://www.iko.com/na/blog/blueprint-for-roofing/) ("add in the dimensions of the roof overhang at rakes and eaves").
- Choice: 1 square = 100 sq ft of roof surface. Bundles per square default to 3 (range 3 to 5). | Source: [GAF Timberline HDZ specs](https://www.gaf.com/en-us/roofing-materials/residential-roofing-materials/shingles/timberline-hdz) (3 bundles per square); IKO lists 3 to 5 bundles per square across its lines ([How many shingles in a bundle](https://www.iko.com/na/blog/how-many-shingles-in-a-bundle/)).
- Choice: Nails per square default to 256 (4 per shingle × 64 shingles; range 100 to 400). Help text notes 384 where 6 nails per shingle is required. Nails = squares with waste × nails per square, rounded up. Starter, ridge cap, and underlayment fasteners are not counted (stated on the page). | Source: [GAF Timberline HDZ specs](https://www.gaf.com/en-us/roofing-materials/residential-roofing-materials/shingles/timberline-hdz).
- Choice *(intake: source upgraded)*: Waste defaults to 10% (range 5 to 25%); help text and copy say 15% or more for hips, valleys, and dormers. Waste applies to field shingles (and so nails) only, not to starter, ridge cap, or underlayment, which come from measured lengths and net coverage. | Source: shingle maker IKO recommends adding 10 to 15 percent depending on roof style ([IKO Blueprint for Roofing](https://www.iko.com/na/blog/blueprint-for-roofing/), [IKO bundle coverage](https://www.iko.com/na/blog/how-many-shingles-in-a-bundle/)); also [Extreme How-To](https://extremehowto.com/calculating-roofing-shingles/).
- Choice: Field bundles before waste = ⌈squares × b/sq⌉; with waste = ⌈squares × (1 + waste) × b/sq⌉. All purchase counts round up to whole units. | Source: shared site rule "always round purchase units up" (shingles are sold by the bundle).
- Choice: Starter = (eave + rake) ÷ starter coverage, rounded up. Rakes included by default; help text says enter 0 if you won't run starter up the rakes. | Source: [GAF Pro-Start data sheet](https://www.gaf.com/en-us/document-library/documents/data-sheets/pro-start-starter-strip-shingles-sell-sheet-resgn546.pdf) ("installs at the eaves and rakes").
- Choice: Hip and ridge cap = (ridge + hip length) ÷ ridge-cap coverage, rounded up. | Source: [GAF Seal-A-Ridge data sheet](https://www.gaf.com/en-us/document-library/documents/data-sheets/seal-a-ridge-ridge-cap-shingles-sell-sheet-reshr112.pdf) (4 bundles ≈ 100 lin ft = 25 per bundle); [OC ProEdge data sheet](https://dcpd6wotaa0mb.cloudfront.net/mdms/dms/Roofing/500378CA/500378CA-ProEdge-Data-Sheet.pdf?v=1564487336000) (33 ft per bundle).
- Choice: Product selector presets (editable). GAF: starter 120.33, ridge 25, underlayment 937.5. Owens Corning: starter 105, ridge 33, underlayment 929. Custom keeps typed values. Default GAF, matching the HDZ defaults. | Sources: [Pro-Start](https://www.gaf.com/en-us/document-library/documents/data-sheets/pro-start-starter-strip-shingles-sell-sheet-resgn546.pdf), [Seal-A-Ridge](https://www.gaf.com/en-us/document-library/documents/data-sheets/seal-a-ridge-ridge-cap-shingles-sell-sheet-reshr112.pdf), [FeltBuster](https://www.gaf.com/en-us/document-library/documents/data-sheets/feltbuster-sell-sheet-resul183_data-sheet.pdf), [Starter Strip Plus](https://www.owenscorning.com/en-us/roofing/components/products/starter-strip-plus), [ProEdge](https://dcpd6wotaa0mb.cloudfront.net/mdms/dms/Roofing/500378CA/500378CA-ProEdge-Data-Sheet.pdf?v=1564487336000), [ProArmor](https://www.owenscorning.com/en-us/roofing/components/products/proarmor).
- Choice *(intake: verified)*: ProEdge 33 lin ft per bundle (12 × 36 in, 22 shingles / 66 pieces, 6 in exposure) confirmed against Owens Corning's own data sheet, Pub. No. 500378-F (March 2019), which replaces the 2017 Lowe's-hosted brochure as the cited source. | Source: [OC ProEdge data sheet](https://dcpd6wotaa0mb.cloudfront.net/mdms/dms/Roofing/500378CA/500378CA-ProEdge-Data-Sheet.pdf?v=1564487336000).
- Choice: Underlayment coverage is net of laps. GAF FeltBuster's rated 1,000 sq ft excludes laps, so the preset applies a 3 in side lap: 1,000 × 45/48 = 937.5 sq ft. OC ProArmor uses its published 929 sq ft with a 3 in overlap. End laps aren't modeled. | Sources: [FeltBuster](https://www.gaf.com/en-us/document-library/documents/data-sheets/feltbuster-sell-sheet-resul183_data-sheet.pdf), [ProArmor](https://www.owenscorning.com/en-us/roofing/components/products/proarmor).
- Choice: Ridge-cap coverage range 15 to 45 lin ft/bundle, so ProEdge's 41.95 ft size fits. | Source: [OC ProEdge data sheet](https://dcpd6wotaa0mb.cloudfront.net/mdms/dms/Roofing/500378CA/500378CA-ProEdge-Data-Sheet.pdf?v=1564487336000).
- Choice *(intake: 2021 → 2024 IRC, wording matched to Roof Pitch)*: Minimum pitch 2/12; below it the pitch (or multiplier) field shows "Below 2/12: the 2024 IRC (R905.2.2) does not allow asphalt shingles on this slope." From 2/12 up to (not including) 4/12 the underlayment count is doubled and a note cites Table R905.1.1(2). Doubling the roll count is a simplification of the IRC's two-layer method. | Source: [2024 IRC Chapter 9 via UpCodes](https://up.codes/viewer/general-services-administration/irc-2024/chapter/9/roof-assemblies) (R905.2.2, Table R905.1.1(2)).
- Choice *(intake)*: Pitch is rounded to 2 decimals before the 2/12 and 4/12 checks, the same rule as the Roof Pitch Calculator, so a pitch handed over from that tool always lands in the same band (e.g. 18.43° = 3.9993/12 → 4/12, single layer). | Source: consistency with roof-pitch-calculator's logged decision ([2024 IRC Table R905.1.1(2)](https://up.codes/viewer/general-services-administration/irc-2024/chapter/9/roof-assemblies)).
- Choice *(intake)*: Cite the 2024 IRC (current edition), not 2021; the page tells readers their local edition and amendments may differ. | Source: [2024 IRC Chapter 9 via UpCodes](https://up.codes/viewer/general-services-administration/irc-2024/chapter/9/roof-assemblies).
- Choice *(intake)*: Drip edge is not counted (no output), but Assumptions now says the 2024 IRC requires it at eaves and rakes, overlapped at least 2 in, so readers add it from their eave and rake lengths. Whether to add it as an output is a scope question (see Open questions). | Source: [2024 IRC R905.2.8.5 via UpCodes](https://up.codes/viewer/general-services-administration/irc-2024/chapter/9/roof-assemblies).
- Choice: Pitch range 2 to 24 (x/12); multiplier 1 to 2.5 with anything under the 2/12 equivalent (about 1.014) rejected; footprint 10 to 200 × 10 to 100 ft; edges 0 to 500 ft. | Source: build brief section 7 ranges; [2024 IRC R905.2.2](https://up.codes/viewer/general-services-administration/irc-2024/chapter/9/roof-assemblies).
- Choice *(intake)*: The `?pitch=` URL parameter (sent by the Roof Pitch Calculator) prefills the pitch field and switches to pitch mode. Value rounded to 2 decimals; missing, empty, negative, or non-numeric values are ignored (default 6 stays); out-of-range numbers pass through so the normal validation message explains them. | Source: most sensible option; matches roof pitch's 2-decimal pitch output.
