## What it calculates and who it's for

This calculator adds up the floor area of up to four rectangular rooms, adds a waste allowance for the layout you're installing, and turns the total into whole boxes using the coverage printed on your flooring's box. It's for anyone buying vinyl plank, laminate, or engineered flooring who wants the box count and how much will be left over.

## How to use

1. **Choose how many rooms** and enter each one's length and width in feet plus inches.
2. **Pick the layout.** Straight and diagonal set the waste for you; for herringbone or another pattern, enter your own percentage.
3. **Enter the square feet per box** from the label.
4. **Add spare boxes** under Advanced if you want stock for repairs.
5. **Read the boxes to buy** and the square feet left over.

## The formula

- Room length or width (ft) = Feet + Inches ÷ 12
- Floor area (sq ft) = Sum of Length × Width for each room
- Area with waste (sq ft) = Floor area × (1 + Waste % ÷ 100)
- Boxes to buy = Area with waste ÷ Coverage per box, **rounded up**, + Spare boxes
- Left over (sq ft) = Boxes × Coverage per box − Area with waste

**Waste %** is 10% for a straight layout and 15% for a diagonal layout, the allowances in Tarkett's ProGen installation instructions. For herringbone, chevron, or any other pattern, the calculator uses the percentage you enter.

## Worked example

Two rooms with diagonal planks and 23.4 sq ft per box: a living room 12 ft 6 in × 10 ft and a hall 8 ft × 8 ft.

1. Living room: 12.5 × 10 = **125 sq ft**; hall: 8 × 8 = **64 sq ft**
2. Floor area: 125 + 64 = **189 sq ft**
3. Diagonal waste, 15%: 189 × 1.15 = **217.35 sq ft**
4. Boxes: 217.35 ÷ 23.4 = 9.29, rounded up to **10 boxes**
5. Left over: 10 × 23.4 = 234 sq ft − 217.35 = **16.65 sq ft**

## How to read your results

- **Boxes to buy** is the headline number. It's rounded up and includes any spare boxes.
- **Left over after waste** is what should remain if the cuts use up the whole waste allowance. Keep leftover planks for repairs; boxes from a later lot can differ in color.
- **Rooms** lists each room's own area so you can check your measurements.

## Assumptions and limits

- Each room is treated as a rectangle. Split L-shaped rooms into two rectangles, and add closets as their own room.
- The 10% and 15% allowances come from one manufacturer's instructions (Tarkett ProGen LVP). Other products may call for different amounts; follow your product's instructions.
- It doesn't estimate underlayment, transition strips, baseboard, or adhesive, and it doesn't cover tile.
- Follow the manufacturer's instructions for acclimation, expansion gaps, and subfloor preparation, and confirm quantities with your supplier.

## FAQ

### How much extra flooring should I buy?

For a straight layout, Tarkett's ProGen instructions say to add 10%; for diagonal, 15%. Rooms with many corners, closets, or angled walls can use more. Patterns like herringbone usually need more again; use the figure from your product's instructions.

### How many boxes of flooring do I need?

Divide the floor area plus waste by the square feet per box on the label and round up. A 12 × 12 ft room in a straight layout with 20 sq ft boxes needs 158.4 ÷ 20 = 7.92, so 8 boxes.

### What is the waste factor for vinyl plank flooring?

For Tarkett's ProGen LVP it's 10% straight and 15% diagonal. Other brands publish their own figures, so check your product's installation guide.

### Should I keep extra flooring after installation?

Many people keep at least one spare box for repairs, because a later production run may not match. Add it under Advanced as an extra box.
